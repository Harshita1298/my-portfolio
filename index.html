About Me

Hi! I'm Harshita Gupta, a passionate Full Stack Developer from Gorakhpur, UP. I completed my Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering with First Division from Govt. Girls Polytechnic, Gorakhpur (BTEUP), and then secured Lateral Entry into BTech CSE 2nd Year at Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gorakhpur University (DDU).

During my Diploma, I did Summer Training at Softpro India (ISO 9001:2015 Certified) on "Java with Spring Framework" — 28 days, Grade A++ (Excellent), and was awarded the prestigious Star Performer Award for being the top performer in my batch. I was also the Batch Head during training. From this training I built two live projects: the OTP Online Test Portal and the Nalanda Open University LMS.

🎓 BTech Journey @ DDU: After lateral entry into 2nd year, I participated in a university hackathon where my team performed really well and ranked in the Top 5. Continuously building projects and growing.

I actively solve problems on LeetCode and hold 3 HackerRank certifications. I run a YouTube channel "Apna Program" where I share source code, tech knowledge, and programming experiences with the community.

I love building real applications — from Android apps (RideCompare, SmratSakhi) to MERN stack e-commerce platforms, real-time PHP chat apps, and full-stack college management systems. 20+ public repos on GitHub.
